About FitNest

Finding reliable health and fitness information can be confusing, especially when everyone's body, goals and lifestyle are different.

FitNest brings essential wellness tools and guidance together in one place. Users can calculate their BMI, estimate their daily water intake, discover personalized workout ideas, explore nutrition suggestions, and find general health guidance.

Our goal is to make health and wellness information simple, accessible, and easier to understand for everyday users.
