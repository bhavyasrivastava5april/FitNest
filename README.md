# 🌿 FitNest

## 📌 About the Project

Maintaining a healthy lifestyle can be difficult when people have access to a huge amount of health and fitness information but don't know what is suitable for them.

Different people have different fitness goals, dietary preferences, activity levels, and health concerns. However, many existing fitness and wellness resources provide generic information rather than simple guidance based on an individual's needs.

FitNest was developed to address this problem by bringing basic health, fitness, nutrition, and wellness tools together in one easy-to-use platform.

The goal of FitNest is to help users get simple and personalized starting points for making healthier lifestyle choices.

> **FitNest is an educational wellness platform and does not replace professional medical advice.**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

People often face difficulties such as:

- Not knowing whether their weight falls within a healthy BMI range.
- Not knowing approximately how much water they should drink.
- Finding it difficult to choose exercises according to their fitness goal and experience level.
- Being confused about what foods may be suitable for a particular fitness goal.
- Finding general health and wellness information scattered across different sources.
- Receiving generic fitness advice that may not match their goals or preferences.

FitNest aims to provide these basic tools and recommendations in a single, simple and responsive website.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

FitNest provides a collection of interactive tools that use user-provided information to generate basic recommendations.

Users can:

1. Calculate their BMI using height and weight.
2. Get an estimated daily water intake.
3. Receive workout suggestions based on their goal, fitness level and available workout duration.
4. Get food suggestions based on their fitness goal, diet preference and meal.
5. View general wellness guidance based on selected health concerns.

The website is designed to be simple enough for beginners while demonstrating how user input can be used to create personalized results.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🧮 BMI Calculator

- Accepts height in centimeters or feet/inches.
- Calculates BMI from height and weight.
- Displays the corresponding BMI category.

### 💧 Water Intake Calculator

- Estimates daily water intake based on body weight and activity level.
- Provides the result in litres.

### 🏋️ Workout Recommendations

Recommendations are based on:

- Weight Loss
- Weight Gain
- Muscle Gain
- General Fitness
- Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced levels
- 15, 30, 45 and 60-minute workout durations

### 🥗 Nutrition Guide

Food suggestions are based on:

- Fitness goal
- Vegetarian or Non-Vegetarian preference
- Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snacks

### 🩺 Health & Wellness Guidance

Provides general educational guidance for:

- Diabetes
- High Blood Pressure
- PCOS
- Joint Problems
- General Wellness
- Other health concerns
